# Linear Design Analysis — adopted for OnsideHR

> Source: voltagent/awesome-design-md → design-md/linear.app/DESIGN.md
> Adopted 2026-09-01 as OnsideHR's design language.
>
> DIVERGENCE FROM SOURCE (deliberate): Linear's system is dark-only. OnsideHR
> is a trust-first, regulated B2B product with a light-first product UI, so we
> adopt Linear's DISCIPLINE — one scarce accent, hairline-over-shadow depth,
> tight display type, no gradients, no spotlight cards, surface ladder — while
> keeping light/dark duality. Accent adapted to Linear's lavender-blue.

## Accent (scarce — CTAs, brand mark, focus ring, link emphasis only)
- Primary:        #5e6ad2
- Primary hover:  #828fff
- Primary focus:  #5e69d1
- On primary:     #ffffff

## Surface ladder (dark canvas, when dark)
- Canvas #010102 · Surface-1 #0f1011 · Surface-2 #141516 · Surface-3 #18191a
- Hairline #23252a · Hairline strong #34343a

## Text
- Ink #f7f8f8 · Ink muted #d0d6e0 · Ink subtle #8a8f98 · Ink tertiary #62666d

## Typography
- Display/Text: Inter (500/600/700 display, 400 body) — Linear's own OSS substitute
- Mono: JetBrains Mono / Geist Mono (400)
- Aggressive negative letter-spacing on display sizes; eyebrow uses +0.4px positive tracking
- Pair display weight 600 with body weight 400

## Radius
- Buttons/inputs 8px · cards 12px · screenshot panels 16px · pills/avatars 9999px
- Do NOT pill-round primary CTAs

## Depth
- Surface ladder, not drop shadows. Hairline borders. No atmospheric gradients, no spotlight cards.

## Do
- Reserve the accent for brand mark, primary CTA, focus ring, link emphasis
- Hairline borders + surface lift for elevation, not heavy shadows
- Display weight 600 + body weight 400, tight negative tracking on display

## Don't
- Introduce secondary chromatic accents
- Add gradients or spotlight-style card treatments
- Pill-round primary CTA buttons
- Use the accent as a section background or card fill
